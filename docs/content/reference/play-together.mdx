---
title: Playing together
description: Play a mod you are building with friends on their own PCs.
---

A friends lobby is a server on your PC that runs your mod. Your friends join it
from their own PCs. On your PC, open a lobby:

```sh
modlock play --lobby
```

On each other PC, join it with the link the lobby shows:

```sh
modlock join LINK
```

Once the player is in the lobby, `join` launches Deadlock and connects to your
server. Guests download the mod's client content from your PC before joining; if
you modify client content afterward, guests must rejoin to download the update.

First sign in on both PCs with `modlock login`, or use the sign-in that
`modlock publish` saved. Only a creator who may publish mods can open a lobby,
and only that creator's friends can join it. The PCs connect directly over
WebRTC. When a router blocks the direct connection, the traffic goes through
Hyperline's relay for as long as the lobby is open.

## Join codes

`modlock play --host` prints a join code instead of opening a lobby, and the
other PC joins with `modlock join CODE`. Anyone who has the code can join until
you stop hosting, but only over a direct connection between the PCs. When
`--lobby` cannot open a lobby, it says why and gives a join code instead. In the
sandbox, nothing is hosted, and you play on your own PC.

## Which mods play

`modlock play` watches one source project, and rebuilds and reloads it each
time you save. It also runs any number of built mods as they are. To play
several source projects together, pass `--watch=false`. Then each project plays
from its existing build, and a project without a build is built first.
