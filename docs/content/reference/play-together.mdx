---
title: Playing together
description: Play a mod you are building with friends on their own PCs.
---

A friends lobby is a server on your PC that runs your mod and that your
friends join from their own PCs. On your PC, open one:

```sh
modlock play --lobby
```

On each other PC, join it with the link the lobby shows:

```sh
modlock join LINK
```

Once the player is in the lobby, `join` launches Deadlock and joins your
server. Each guest downloads the mod's content from your PC before joining. A
guest sees content you change after that only when they join again.

First sign in on both PCs with `modlock login`, or use the sign-in that
`modlock publish` saved. Only a creator who may publish mods can open a
lobby, and only that creator's friends can join it. The PCs connect directly
over WebRTC, and when a router blocks the direct connection, the traffic goes
through Hyperline's relay for as long as the lobby is open.

## Join codes

`modlock play --host` prints a join code instead of opening a lobby, and the
other PC joins with `modlock join CODE`. Anyone who has the code can join until
you stop hosting, but only over a direct connection between the PCs. When `--lobby` cannot open a lobby, it says why and
gives a join code instead. In the sandbox, nothing is hosted and you play on
your own PC.

## Which mods play

`modlock play` watches one source project, which it rebuilds and reloads each
time you save, and runs any number of built mods as they are. To play several
source projects together, pass `--watch=false`: each project then plays from its
existing build, which is made first if the project has none.
